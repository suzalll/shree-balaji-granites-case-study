# Shree Balaji Granites | Wholesale catalogue

## Overview

A wholesale catalogue website for Shree Balaji Granites, presenting architectural materials for trade buyers in Rajasthan, India.

## My role

AI-assisted website development and presentation of the catalogue and enquiry journey.

## What the site presents

- 40 designs across the Suncore and Yellow Florex ranges.
- An enquiry-oriented journey: choose designs, state quantities and location, then request a quotation.
- Content aimed at dealers, distributors, builders, contractors and fabricators.

## Scope and limits

This case study documents the website presentation. It does not claim a factory stock-management system, completed customer orders or measured business results. The site's room scenes are labelled conceptual visualizations, not photographs of verified installations. Enquiry delivery and business fulfilment are not demonstrated by this case study.
